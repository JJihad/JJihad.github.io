Who uses this app

This app is used by one person only, its owner. It is not available to other users.

Google user data accessed

The app requests the Google Drive scope "drive.file". With this scope it can see and modify only the files and folders that the app itself creates in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot read, list or change any other file in the account.

How the data is used

The app uploads encrypted backup archives on a weekly schedule, lists its own uploaded files, and deletes its own older archives. The files are encrypted before upload, so their contents are not readable by the app or by Google.

Storage, sharing and retention

The OAuth token is stored on the owner's own server. No data is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising. Only the newest archives are kept; older ones are deleted by the app.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the Google Account security settings and can delete the backup files directly in Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
